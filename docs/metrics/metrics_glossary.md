# Brightcart Metrics Glossary

Owner: Analytics team

Standard definitions for the KPIs reported to leadership. Use these when building dashboards or answering metric questions.

## Refund Rate

**Definition:** The share of order revenue that was refunded.

```
Refund Rate = SUM(refund_amount) / SUM(order amount) for orders in the period
```

- Captures the financial impact of refunds, so large refunds weigh more than small ones.
- Reported as a percentage with one decimal place.

## Monthly Active Users (MAU)

**Definition:** The number of distinct users with at least one session in the trailing **28 days**.

## Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition:** Total order amount divided by the number of orders in the period.

## Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)

**Definition:** The total amount of all orders placed in the period.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Definition:** Total marketing spend divided by the number of new customers who placed a first order in the period.
